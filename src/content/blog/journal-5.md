CS 61B is an interesting class because you learn a lot of the materials through coding even though a lot of the concepts (Like abstract data types) are theoretical. You’ll learn a wide range of data structures from arrays to heaps to binary search trees as well as the ways of analyzing efficiency and feasibility of algorithms and ideas. I took The BL version during the summer(B and Lab), so it’s a lab-heavy class with 4 labs per week. As painful as it was for me to finish the assignments within the two hours each time, they help solidify my understanding in the concepts and inspiration behind algorithms and data structures. This class will prepare you do to leetcode problems(easy-mediums), as most problems that seem difficult at first contain concepts you will learn throughout the course. My advice for doing well in the class is the same as for CS 61A, and it is encouraged that you ask your TAs as many questions as you can because many exam-level questions require a deep understanding of the topics chosen.
