Having done physics and mathematics for a while, I get this thought in the back of my head: “why is this subject so hard?”. While this question is trivial given my situation where I have to learn and derive many concepts and examples, an idea came to me which led me to the idea of the difference between a person in the state of “dreaming” and of “doing”. The only difference is the willingness to take risk. It’s easy to scoff at this simplistic idea that taking risks will get someone through the whole journey when sources around us say that taking risks enables people to only discover a new path of perspective. If you look closely at the concept of risk relative to doing things rather than dreaming, the risk includes the whole journey. How do you know if doing this is the most optimal? How do you know if you really can be where you are if you do this? These external questions although we don’t necessarily think about would at times creep up to us. Consider the concept of going on a roller coaster. Many people think that going in the line and in the roller coaster is a risk that people take, but the real risk is the whole coaster. Following the same format of the question: how do you know if the roller coaster doesn’t malfunction and that you won’t lose your life from it? To truly embrace the journey is to embrace the uncertainty, and overcoming these questions will be instrumental in transitioning from being the dreamer to the doer
