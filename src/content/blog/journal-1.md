In the age of AI, there have been more existence of AI-assisted(or made) writings on the internet, whether that’s in email responses, google gemini responses whenever you search up something in the search bar, or in LinkedIn posts. While I agree that AI can be a useful tool for humans just how calculators allowed students to focus more on the ideas behind math than having to do elementary calculations over and over again, I can’t help but notice a change that I believe can hinder people’s ability to think just how social media have “reduced” our attention spans.There’s been some dependence on online chatbots for many of the daily tasks we would consider normal before LLM’s were even a thing, such as emailing someone back and writing essays. While using AI for these tasks takes effort off of the mind which can be used to focus on other things, the action itself can lead to less intimate conversations online which could potentially ruin work or life relationships. LLMs on the outside takes a lot of information off of the internet and computes the “average”(whatever the black box does) response of a given prompt, which is great for information, but terrible for expression of emotions. With billions of people on the internet nowadays, the amount of data points you get from the web is significant in comparison to the works of past authors who may have spent a whole lifetime developing stories that are timeless and enjoyable. This difference results in emotionless writing, or text that has a shell but has nothing inside. I would like to point out that machine learning is the art of prediction models, meaning they could predict what human-felt emotions could look like on text, but they don’t have any concept or experience of the actual essence. Therefore, if AI is used to write responses or informational text online, that would only result in passionless writing that won’t give someone the “itch” or goosebump they would have if they listened to a great song or read great literature. This brings into the main point, which is the question of why should we keep thinking if we are planning to let some external thing dictate that we say or think. Though this question is absurd since we are thinking creatures with an ability to think in high abstract level in comparison to other animals, it does bring up a point where the whole point of writing or creating is to solidify experiences in our lives which can be shared with other people. Memes is a good example because it combines jest/satire/jokes with an experience that can be relatable. Don’t get me wrong, LLMs are great for findinginformation just how search bars are great for us humans to find relevant information, it’s the fact that LLMs can creep into our world of expression that is something I find deeply concerning which I would like to share. Such action creates another layer between you and your work and that ruins the point of having fun with creating. It’s like asking your friend to build a lego set for you instead of you going through the actual instructions ot build it yourself. So next time you write that next essay or email or that linkedin post, maybe consider actually writing about something that truly amazes you about this topic. Trust me, the internet will be a much better place for inspiration and passion-building if it’s filled with human writing than summaries that all look the same and emotionless when there can be.
